      Scale complex

      A key under the fingers - scale, arpeggio, tonic chord in three positions, cadence - played from the page, both hands.

      Progressions

      A progression, each chord moving as little as it can to the next, in one key or round the circle - played from the page.

      Transposition

      Three phrases, each played as written and then in another key on the keys - the new key's notes show as you find them.

      Intervals

      Ten intervals: hear one and play it back on the keys from the note given, or hear or read one and tap its name.

      Chords

      Ten chords - major, minor, diminished, augmented, and where asked their position or sevenths: read or hear one, and tap its name.

      Key signatures

      Ten key signatures: read one, and tap the key it is - major, minor, or either, as the question says.

      Functions

      Six progressions of four chords: read or hear one, and tap each chord's numeral in the key.

      Cadences

      Six phrases of four chords: read or hear one, and tap the cadence it ends on - authentic, plagal, half or deceptive.

      Melodic dictation

      Four phrases, each after its key's cadence: hear one, and play it back on the keys from the note given, or tap each note's degree in the key.

      Rhythmic dictation

      Eight bars of rhythm: hear one, and tap it back in time on any key.
